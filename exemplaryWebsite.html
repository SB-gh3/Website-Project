Why the BBC is a well designed website

The BBC News website is well designed because it has a clear layout that is mobile friendly and adapts to different screen sizes. The design changes smoothly depending on the user’s device, making the site easy to use on both large and small screens.

The links on the website are easy to see and click, which improves navigation and helps users quickly find the information they need. On one hand, the main headline stays at the centre of the page and remains visible when the screen size becomes smaller. On the other hand, secondary headlines and subtitles are hidden when the screen gets smaller to reduce clutter and improve readability. Finally, the layout changes from three columns to two columns, and then to a single column on mobile devices, ensuring the content stays organized and easy to follow.

Some benefits of this website:

  1. Mobile friendly design
  2. Main headline stays visible on all screen sizes
  3. Links are easy to see and click
  4. The layout continues to adapt as the screen gets smaller
  5. The design is visually eye-catching for users
