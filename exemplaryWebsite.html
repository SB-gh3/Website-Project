What Makes a Website Well or Poorly Designed?

A well designed website prioritizes clarity, accessibility, and usability. Content should be easy to read, navigation should feel intuitive, and the layout should adapt smoothly to different screen sizes. When these elements work together, users can find information quickly without feeling overwhelmed or confused.

In contrast, a poorly designed website often suffers from cluttered layouts, inconsistent styling, or unclear navigation. These issues can frustrate users and make important information hard to locate, even if the content itself is valuable. Over time, this may cause visitors to leave the site earlier then intended.
