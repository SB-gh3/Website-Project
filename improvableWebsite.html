Why the Drudge Report is a poorly designed website

The Drudge Report is a poorly designed website because it lacks consistency and ease of navigation. The webpage looks very messy, with no clear or consistent layout. It contains many links that are tightly stacked together, making them difficult to read and navigate. In addition, the website uses several different fonts, which makes the page look unattractive and unorganized. The placement of advertisements also feels random, further adding to the overall clutter and confusion of the site.
